# Support

- **Questions:** the [FAQ](../faq.md) answers common ones, and the [manual](../manual.md) explains the app.
- **Problems and ideas:** open an issue on [GitHub]($repo$/issues). In the app, ⚙ **Settings**, *Logging*,
  *Report a problem* prepares a report that you can attach after looking through it.
- **The radio itself:** building, buying and the firmware of the kv4p HT are covered by the
  [kv4p HT project](https://www.kv4p.com).

Issues are public: leave out anything you do not want to share, such as your position.
