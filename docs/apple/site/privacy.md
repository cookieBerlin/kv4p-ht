# Privacy

**DB3AC, the developer of kv4p HT (by DB3AC), collects no data.** The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking,
and it sends nothing to the developer.

## The app

What the app sends leaves your device only as you set it up, and goes to the radio, to the APRS network or to
your own iCloud account:

- **Radio:** the microphone is only on while you transmit, and its audio goes only to the radio.
- **APRS:** packets you transmit, or send to the public APRS-IS network, contain your callsign and, if you turn
  them on, position beacons with your position, rounded as you choose. With the iGate on, the packets your radio
  receives go to APRS-IS too.
- **Location:** read only for position beacons, the APRS-IS feed, or the distances in the APRS list.
- **iCloud:** with *iCloud sync* on, your memories, callsign and a few settings are kept in your own iCloud
  account, to reach your other devices. Apple's privacy policy applies to it.
- **Logs and problem reports:** the app keeps a log on your device, without callsigns, message texts or
  positions. A report leaves your device only if you share it yourself.

The [FAQ](../faq.md#does-the-app-collect-personal-data) has the details. TestFlight, which delivers the test
builds, may send Apple crash reports and feedback that you choose to share; Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

## This website

The website has no cookies, no analytics and no content from other servers. A colour scheme chosen with its switch is
kept in your browser only, and never leaves it. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which
may log the addresses of visitors for security; see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
